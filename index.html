Before going live

This dashboard is a front-end prototype. It has no authentication, server, database, payment processing or shared records. All sample names, counts and amounts are illustrative.

Secure sign-in and permissions

Admin, teacher and student roles must be validated on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

Privacy and safeguarding

Use secure storage, access controls, audit logs, backups and clear retention rules for student records.

Integrations

Connect admissions, attendance, homework, complaints, messaging and fee records to one database.
